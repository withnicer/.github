Nicer is a travel platform and advisor community. We build tools that help independent travel advisors design personalized trips, book travel, and stay connected with their clients from the first conversation through the trip itself.

## For travel advisors

Manage client profiles and conversations, build and share itineraries, search and book travel, and collect payments in the Advisor Portal. The AI-assisted itinerary builder helps turn trip details into a proposal you can share as a web link, in the app, or as a PDF.

[Become a Nicer advisor](https://join.nicer.travel/) · [Read the advisor guides](https://docs.nicer.travel/advisors/)

## For travelers

Work with a travel advisor to plan a trip that fits you. The Nicer app brings your itinerary, booking details, and conversation with your advisor together.

[Meet our advisors](https://www.nicer.travel/advisors) · [Download the Nicer app](https://www.nicer.travel/download)

## For developers

The Nicer API supports travel workflows from inventory search and quotes to bookings, shared itineraries, and payments. The Nicer MCP server lets advisors connect compatible AI clients to their Nicer workspace.

[Explore the API documentation](https://docs.nicer.travel/developers/) · [Connect to Nicer with MCP](https://docs.nicer.travel/developers/mcp/)
